Finite Element Analysis of Reinforced Concrete Deep Beams

Compared structural reinforcement configurations and behavior of beams with large openings using Abaqus.

AbaqusFinite elementsSimulation

Project overview

This collaborative research used finite element analysis to investigate strengthening reinforced-concrete deep beams with openings using metal plates.

FEAmethod
2023related publication
Case 3preferred configuration in the reported analysis

Methods & workflow

  1. Created numerical models of beams with large openings in Abaqus.
  2. Compared different orientations and configurations of strengthening metal plates.
  3. Evaluated deformation, deflection and load-carrying behavior under the modeled conditions.
  4. Considered practical material choices and associated costs.

Results & takeaways

The project identified a preferred plate configuration, labeled case 3 in the reported comparison, on the basis of structural response and load-carrying performance.

Scope and interpretation. This is a numerical-analysis summary, not a construction recommendation or a substitute for code-compliant engineering design.
