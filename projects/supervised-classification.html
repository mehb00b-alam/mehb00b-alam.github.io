Supervised Classification & Decision Boundary Analysis

Implemented K-nearest neighbors and multiclass least-squares classification algorithms from scratch in Julia.

Methods and scope

Tested Double Moon, Concentric Gaussian, and Gaussian XOR datasets, comparing K=1,5,15, varied sample sizes and nonlinear decision boundaries.

Results & status

The CV reports 97.2% training accuracy on Gaussian data and 100%, 96.25%, and 99% on three least-squares classification datasets, respectively. These are training accuracies, not held-out results.
