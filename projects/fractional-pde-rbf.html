Numerical Modeling of Fractional PDEs with RBF Collocation

Developed MATLAB numerical approximations for time-fractional PDEs with meshfree radial basis functions and time-stepping.

MATLABRBF collocationFinite differences

Project overview

The GIK Institute M.S. thesis focused on numerical approximation of fractional partial differential equations with meshfree radial basis function collocation, supported by finite-difference time discretization.

2024M.S. thesis completed
2−αtemporal scheme convergence exponent
2023related published article

Methods & workflow

  1. Studied radial basis function families including multiquadric, inverse multiquadric, inverse quadric and Gaussian functions.
  2. Approximated spatial dependence through collocation at scattered centers rather than using a conventional mesh.
  3. Combined RBF spatial approximation with a fractional time-stepping scheme of theoretical order O(Δt^(2−α)).
  4. Implemented numerical experiments in MATLAB and evaluated approximation errors and convergence.
  5. Published work on a time-fractional FitzHugh–Nagumo equation in Fractal and Fractional (2023).

Results & takeaways

The simulations investigated approximation accuracy and stability for test problems in fractional dynamics. This work connects differential equations and numerical linear algebra with scientific computing applications.

Scope and interpretation. Exact simulation plots and code are not included in this portfolio release; the related publication provides the formal research result.
