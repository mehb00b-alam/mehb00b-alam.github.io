Thesis · 2024

Radial basis function collocation method for fractional partial differential equations

An investigation of numerical approximation methods for fractional PDEs, with meshfree radial basis functions for spatial representation and a finite-difference scheme in time.

GIK Institute of Engineering Sciences and Technology

Research overview

An investigation of numerical approximation methods for fractional PDEs, with meshfree radial basis functions for spatial representation and a finite-difference scheme in time.

Computational formulation

Radial basis function collocation approximates functions using combinations of radial kernels at chosen centers. Spatial collocation avoids conventional grid connectivity; the time-fractional component is advanced using a discrete temporal scheme.

RBF families & experiments

The research studied multiquadric, inverse multiquadric, inverse quadric and Gaussian radial basis functions and investigated shape parameters, accuracy and numerical stability.

Results and output

Numerical examples were used to measure approximation error and convergence; the related paper studied the time-fractional FitzHugh–Nagumo equation.
