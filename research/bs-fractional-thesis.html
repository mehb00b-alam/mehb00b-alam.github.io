Thesis · 2020

Stability analysis of impulsive fractional and q-fractional implicit differential equations

A study of qualitative properties of fractional-order and q-fractional implicit differential equations, including systems with boundary and nonlocal integral conditions.

University of Peshawar

Research overview

A study of qualitative properties of fractional-order and q-fractional implicit differential equations, including systems with boundary and nonlocal integral conditions.

Problem setting

The thesis considered fractional and q-fractional equations, including impulsive systems and nonlocal boundary conditions, within fractional differential-equation models.

Analytical approach

Banach’s contraction principle, Krasnoselskii’s theorem and related fixed-point tools were used to investigate existence and uniqueness; Hyers–Ulam and Hyers–Ulam–Rassias methods supported stability analysis.

Research continuation

Work in this area contributed to peer-reviewed research involving Caputo, Riemann–Liouville and Hadamard fractional operators.
