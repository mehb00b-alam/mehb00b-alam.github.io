A little about me.

✳

My work sits at the intersection of applied mathematics, machine learning, and real-world problem solving.

I've spent years studying numerical methods for differential equations, publishing research, and teaching mathematics. More recently, I've brought that foundation into practical machine-learning projects—building clustering and regression algorithms, exploring model generalization, and learning how to turn rigorous mathematical thinking into useful applications.

I'm especially interested in data science, machine learning, quantitative analytics, operations research, and scientific computing. I enjoy working on problems where thoughtful modeling and careful experimentation can make a measurable difference.
